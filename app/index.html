    Roadmap to the platinum

    Six stages, in the order to play them. Every stage is built from the official trophy descriptions: story chapters and missions first, then the side strands, then activities, then heat and heists, then money and the map — and 100% completion last, which unlocks the platinum.

    Missable trophies — 18 to watch

    These trophies are tied to a story beat or a choice. The official descriptions do not say the moment can be replayed, so earn each one when it comes up instead of leaving it for later. Everything else in the list can be finished after the story, at your own pace.
